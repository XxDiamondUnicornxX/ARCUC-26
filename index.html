About Us

Hello everyone!

We're the official collegiate chapter of the American Red Cross here at UChicago. We host lots of events on campus, like banana suturing, CPR workshops, events during health awareness week, and more. We also have a buddy system that pairs pre-med students with upperclassmen, board members and med students, plus plenty of ways to give back to the local community, like blood drives, Sound the Alarm and volunteering.

Have questions about pre-med, or health in general? Reach out at americanredcrossofuchicago@gmail.com. We look forward to working with you!

Our Mission

We are dedicated to promoting safety and support through various initiatives, including CPR certifications, peer mentoring, and community service opportunities. Our mission is to empower individuals and foster connections by organizing engaging events like fundraising drives and quarterly blood drives. Join us in making a positive impact in our community!

Founding Journey

The American Red Cross at UChicago was established with a mission to serve the community and make a positive impact through various initiatives and projects. Our journey began with a small group of passionate individuals dedicated to helping those in need.

Expanding Horizons

With time, our club grew and expanded its reach to encompass a wider range of community service programs, medical mentoring opportunities, and life-saving CPR/blood donation events. We have consistently evolved to meet the changing needs of our community.

Impactful Initiatives

Our club has successfully implemented numerous projects focused on community welfare, pre-medical education, and emergency response training. Each initiative is carefully planned to bring about meaningful change and support those in crisis.

Commitment to Service

We remain committed to our core values of compassion, integrity, and service excellence. Our dedication to helping others and fostering a culture of giving back drives our ongoing commitment to community welfare and humanitarian efforts.

Future Vision

Looking ahead, we aspire to continue expanding our reach, engaging more volunteers, and implementing innovative programs that address the pressing needs of our local and global communities.
