Community Service

Showing up for our community

From our campus to hospitals across Chicago, our members volunteer throughout the year. No experience needed — just bring yourself and a willingness to help.

  • Writing get-well and holiday cards for hospital patients
  • Stress-relief and wellness tabling on campus
  • Fundraising drives supporting Red Cross disaster relief
  • CPR and first aid certification trainings

Sound the Alarm

Help make homes safer from fire

Home fires are one of the most common disasters the Red Cross responds to. Through Sound the Alarm, our volunteers team up with the American Red Cross to go door to door in Chicago neighborhoods, installing free smoke alarms and helping families prepare.

  • Install free smoke alarms in homes that need them
  • Share fire safety tips with families
  • Help households create a home fire escape plan
  • No experience needed — training is provided on the day
