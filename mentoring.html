Pre-Med Mentoring

Join our pre-med mentoring

Hey there! Want to level up your pre-med journey? Sign up to get one-on-one mentoring with upperclassmen, our awesome board members and med students. You'll get personalized tips, support, and all the guidance you need to thrive in the medical field.

  • A buddy (an upperclassman, board member or med student) who's been through it
  • Help figuring out classes and juggling the workload
  • Tips on finding research, shadowing and clinical hours
  • Honest advice about the MCAT and applying to med school
Families

Find your people

When you join, you're put in a family: a small group to study with, grab food with, and lean on when the quarter gets busy. Pre-med is a lot easier with friends who get it.

📚

Study Together

Tackle the tough pre-med classes together instead of alone.

🍕

Family Socials

Food, study breaks and hangouts. Because you need a break sometimes!

🌱

Pay It Forward

Once you've found your footing, you can be a mentor for someone else.

How It Works

How to get started

  1. Sign Up

    Fill out the mentee or mentor form. It only takes a few minutes.

  2. Get Matched

    We'll pair you with an upperclassman, board member or med student based on your interests and goals.

  3. Meet Your Family

    Get to know your family and your new support system.

  4. Keep in Touch

    Check in with your mentor and come to family events all year.

Ready to join a family?

Sign up as a mentee, or give back as a mentor. Either way, we're excited to meet you!
