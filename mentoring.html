Pre-Med Mentoring

Learn from those a few steps ahead

The pre-med path can feel overwhelming. Our mentoring program pairs students with upperclassmen who have been through it — so you have someone to ask about classes, research, clinical hours, volunteering and preparing for medical school.

  • One-on-one guidance from an upperclassman mentor
  • Advice on course planning and balancing the pre-med workload
  • Tips for finding research, shadowing and clinical opportunities
  • Insight into MCAT prep and the application process
  • CPR certification and hands-on health & safety training through the club
Families

More than a mentor — a family

Every mentee and mentor is placed into a small “family” that mixes students across years. Families meet up throughout the year for study sessions, meals and socials, so you build a network of friends who understand the journey.

📚

Study Together

Group study sessions and shared resources for the toughest pre-med courses.

🍕

Family Socials

Dinners, study breaks and events to unwind and get to know each other.

🌱

Grow as a Leader

Mentees become mentors — pass on what you've learned to the next class.

How It Works

Getting started

  1. Apply

    Fill out the mentee or mentor interest form at the start of the year.

  2. Get Matched

    We pair you with a mentor based on your interests and goals.

  3. Meet Your Family

    Join your family at our kickoff event and meet your new support system.

  4. Stay Connected

    Check in with your mentor and attend family events throughout the year.
